Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Knightfall is designed with user privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the control you have over your data.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Depending on how you use Knightfall (the Android application or the web client), we may collect and process the following data:

  • Account Information: If you sign up using email or Google Sign-In, we store your email address, user ID, and username in Firebase Authentication and Firestore to secure your account and sync your stats.
  • Game Data: If you use an account or play as a guest in matchmaking/friend play, we collect game states, chess moves, and outcomes. This is required to facilitate online play and track your Elo rating.
  • Leaderboard Profiles: Profiles are private by default. If you opt-in to a Public profile via the app settings, your username, Elo rating, and win/loss records will be visible on the in-app leaderboard and this website.
  • Diagnostics and Analytics: In the Android application, we collect anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics), and performance metrics (Firebase Performance Monitoring) to improve stability and gameplay. Each of these components is fully optional.

2. Data Running on Your Device

Your games played against the local AI, and comments generated by the on-device AI coach, run entirely offline on your phone's processor. This data is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.

3. Your Rights & Choices

We believe you should have total control over your data. Knightfall provides the following options directly inside the app's Settings screen:

  • Toggle Public Profile: Switch your profile between public and private at any time.
  • Opt-out of Diagnostics: Turn off crash reporting, usage analytics, or performance tracking individually.
  • Delete Account and Data: One tap permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication record, Firestore profile, and online game history. This action cannot be undone.

4. Ads & Monetization

Knightfall displays Google AdMob banner ads at the bottom of the screens and interstitial ads before games start. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers and cookies for ad targeting and personalization, subject to your device settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback about our privacy practices, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository.
