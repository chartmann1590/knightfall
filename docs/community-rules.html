Knightfall Community Rules

Knightfall's online play, public profiles, and leaderboard are shared spaces. These rules apply to everyone who plays online or turns on a public profile.

The rules

  • Usernames and profiles: no offensive, hateful, harassing, or impersonating names. Don't pretend to be Knightfall staff, another player, or a public figure.
  • Fair play: no cheating — that includes using an engine or outside assistance in rated online games against other people.
  • Respect other players: no harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse of any kind.
  • No spam or scams: don't use profiles or games to advertise, phish, or defraud other players.

Reporting and blocking

You can report or block any player from the leaderboard and from inside an online game (tap the ⋮ menu next to the player):

  • Report player — pick a reason (inappropriate username, cheating, harassment, or other). Reports are sent to the developer and reviewed.
  • Block player — blocked players disappear from your leaderboard and you won't be matched against them in quick match.

Enforcement

Reports are reviewed by the developer. Players who break these rules can have their public profile removed, their account restricted, or be removed from Knightfall entirely, depending on the severity. If you believe a decision was made in error, reach out via the GitHub repository.

See also the Privacy Policy.
