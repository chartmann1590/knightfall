Your privacy, your call

Knightfall is built to respect you:

  • Guest mode — play everything without creating an account.
  • Public profiles are opt-in — you only appear on the leaderboard and this website if you flip the switch yourself.
  • Crash reports, analytics, performance data and notifications can each be turned off in Settings at any time.
  • The AI and coach run on your phone — your games against the computer never leave your device.
  • Delete everything — one tap in Settings removes your account and all of your data.
